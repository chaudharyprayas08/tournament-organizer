Pool A 5 players · 4 matches each

📊 Ranking & Tiebreaker Rules

Legend: P=Played · W=Wins · L=Losses · PF=Points For · PA=Points Against

Sort order:
  1. Points — Win = 2, Loss = 0
  2. Win% = W / P
  3. NRR = (PF − PA) / P
  4. PR = PF / PA
  5. PF — total points scored

Top 3 → QF Pools. 4th & 5th enter the Wildcard race.

Semis & Final

🏆 Qualifier Method (IPL-style)

S1=QA winner, S2=QB winner, S3=QA runner-up, S4=QB runner-up.
Q1: S1 vs S2 → winner to Final
Eliminator: S3 vs S4 → loser out
Q2: Q1 loser vs Elim winner → winner to Final
Final: Q1 winner vs Q2 winner 🏆
